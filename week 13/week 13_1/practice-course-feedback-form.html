Course Evaluation Form

The end-of-semester evaluation for Web Programming, split into 4 short steps with Back / Next and built from every common input type: name and a 9-digit student ID (or an anonymous switch that skips both), a 1–5 star overall rating, 14 agreement statements grouped into Course Design, Teaching, Assignments & Feedback and Learning Outcomes, a difficulty slider, pace, study time, what helped most, a recommend question, the semester’s 12 topics (Week 2 HTML to Week 13 React Forms) and four comment boxes with live 300-character counters. Submitting validates the form and thanks the student by name. Responses are saved in this browser and added up into a results summary that can be exported as CSV. Bonus: Submit stays disabled until every required question is answered.
