Course Feedback Form

A feedback form with every common input type: text, an 8-digit student ID, star-rating radio buttons, topic checkboxes and a comments box with a live 300-character counter. Submitting validates the form and then thanks the student by name. Bonus: the submit button stays disabled while any required field is empty.
