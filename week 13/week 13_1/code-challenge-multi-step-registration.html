Multi-Step Registration

A 4-step registration wizard: Personal Info, Account Setup, Preferences, then Review & Confirm. All answers live in one formData object in the parent, so going back with Previous never loses what you typed. Each step is validated on every keystroke and Next stays disabled until the step is valid. Extras: a digits-only 10-digit student ID, a live password-strength meter with a rules checklist, and a review step with Edit links and a required terms checkbox. The final submit runs console.log(fullFormObject), mirrored in the console panel beside the form.
