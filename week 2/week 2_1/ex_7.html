How to Make Perfect Pancakes

What You Need

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 tablespoon butter

Steps

  1. Mix the dry ingredients in a large bowl.
  2. In a separate bowl, whisk the milk, egg, and melted butter.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and gently fold them together.
  4. Cook on a hot pan for about 2 minutes per side.
  5. Serve with syrup and enjoy!
