alt Text Deep Dive

<img alt="...">

The alt attribute on <img> elements is the most well-known accessibility feature — and the most frequently misused. The goal is to provide a text equivalent conveying the same informational value as the image: describe the purpose it serves, not just what it looks like.

HTML Best Practices 
<!-- ❌ Missing alt (WCAG failure — worst case) -->
<img src="profile.jpg">

<!-- ❌ Generic / useless alt -->
<img src="profile.jpg" alt="image">

<!-- ✅ Informational image -->
<img src="profile.jpg" alt="Kim Jisoo, Web Programming student">

<!-- ✅ Decorative image — empty alt tells SR to skip -->
<img src="wave.svg" alt="">

<!-- ✅ Linked logo — describes destination -->
<a href="/"><img src="logo.png" alt="Gachon University homepage"></a>
