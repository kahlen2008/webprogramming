The <button> vs <div> Problem

<button type="button">

One of the most common accessibility mistakes is using a <div> or <span> with an onclick handler instead of a native <button>. Visually they look identical — but to assistive technology and keyboard users, they are completely different.

HTML Comparison 
<!-- ❌ div as button — BROKEN for a11y -->
<div class="btn" onclick="submitForm()">
  Submit
</div>

<!-- Problems:
  - Not reachable by Tab key
  - Screen reader: "Submit" (no button role)
  - No Enter / Space activation
  - No disabled attribute support -->

<!-- ✅ real <button> — WORKS FOR EVERYONE -->
<button type="button" onclick="submitForm()">
  Submit
</button>
