ARIA Attributes

aria-label · aria-describedby · aria-live

ARIA (Accessible Rich Internet Applications) communicates semantic meaning to assistive technology when HTML alone is not sufficient. Remember The First Rule of ARIA: Don't use ARIA if a native HTML element already exists.

HTML Patterns 
<!-- aria-label: icon-only button -->
<button aria-label="Search">
  <svg aria-hidden="true">...</svg>
</button>

<!-- aria-describedby: form field with hint -->
<label for="email">Email address</label>
<input type="email" id="email"
       aria-describedby="email-hint">
<p id="email-hint">We will send a verification link.</p>

<!-- aria-expanded: accordion pattern -->
<button aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="faq-1">
  What is accessibility?
</button>
<div id="faq-1" hidden>...</div>
