2 What I Changed (Code Snippets: Before vs. After)

Step 2 & Step 3 Code Diffs

01 Missing <main> Landmark (Direct Lighthouse Failure)

WCAG 2.4.1 Fixed
Problem: The page encapsulated primary content inside a non-semantic <div class="main-container">. Screen readers could not detect a primary landmark, triggering the Lighthouse audit failure: "Document does not have a main landmark."
Remediation: Replaced the generic container with a semantic <main class="main-container" id="main-content"> element so assistive technology can directly target the body content.
❌ Before (Failing Div) week 9_1/index.html 
<!-- Generic div communicates no landmark role -->
<div class="main-container">
  <div class="content-grid">
    ...
  </div>
</div>
✅ After (Semantic <main>) week 9_1/index.html 
<!-- Screen readers announce 'main landmark' -->
<main class="main-container" id="main-content">
  <div class="content-grid">
    ...
  </div>
</main>

02 Navigation Landmark & Decorative Vector Hiding

WCAG 1.3.1 Fixed
Problem: The top bar was rendered as an untyped <div class="topbar"> without a landmark navigation role. Furthermore, inline SVG graphics had no accessible names or decorative suppression, causing screen readers to announce unlabelled graphic paths.
Remediation: Upgraded to <nav class="topbar" aria-label="Course Navigation"> and marked decorative SVGs with aria-hidden="true".
❌ Before week 9_1/index.html 
<div class="topbar">
  <a href="../../index.html" class="back-link">
    <svg width="18">...</svg> Course Hub
  </a>
</div>
✅ After week 9_1/index.html 
<nav class="topbar" aria-label="Course Navigation">
  <a href="../../index.html" class="back-link">
    <svg aria-hidden="true">...</svg> Course Hub
  </a>
</nav>

03 Section Groupings & Heading Hierarchy

WCAG 1.3.1 / 2.4.6 Fixed
Problem: Lecture Topics and Practice Exercise sections used non-semantic <div class="section-card"> containers with generic <div class="section-title"> styling. Users relying on screen readers skim pages by cycling through <h1> → <h2> tags.
Remediation: Wrapped both columns in semantic <section aria-labelledby="..."> landmarks and upgraded their titles to formal <h2> headings.
❌ Before week 9_1/index.html 
<div class="section-card">
  <div class="section-title">Practice Exercises</div>
  ...
</div>
✅ After week 9_1/index.html 
<section class="section-card" aria-labelledby="ex-head">
  <h2 class="section-title" id="ex-head">Practice Exercises</h2>
  ...
</section>

04 Accessible Skip Navigation Link & Text Contrast

WCAG 2.4.1 / 1.4.3 Fixed
Problem: Keyboard-only navigators were forced to tab repeatedly through all top-level links before reaching the grid, and subdued caption text (#64748b) had borderline contrast ratios.
Remediation: Added a focus-activated <a href="#main-content" class="skip-link"> at the top of the document and improved text colors to high-contrast #94a3b8 (> 7:1 ratio).
❌ Before week 9_1/index.html 
<body>
  <!-- Keyboard navigators forced to tab through entire topbar -->
  <nav class="topbar">...</nav>
</body>
✅ After week 9_1/index.html 
<body>
  <a href="#main-content" class="skip-link">
    Skip to main content
  </a>
  <nav class="topbar">...</nav>
</body>
